This page was removed on purpose.

Not a broken link and not a server fault. The page that lived at this address documented a capability Observer Protocol has retired, and it was taken down rather than left to mislead someone who followed it.

There is no direct replacement, and pointing you at one would be its own inaccuracy. The pages removed here described agent reputation scoring. That is no longer part of what the protocol offers, so the honest answer is that the thing you were looking for is not somewhere else on this site under a new name.

What Observer Protocol does now is narrower and easier to check: a mandate a person signs, a refusal when a request falls outside it, and a signed record you can verify without asking us for anything.
